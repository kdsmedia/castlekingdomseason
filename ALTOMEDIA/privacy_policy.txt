2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

2.1 Information Collected Automatically

When you play Castle Kingdom Season, certain information is collected automatically by the Game and by third-party SDKs integrated into the Game. This information includes:

a) Device Information
   - Device model, manufacturer, and hardware specifications
   - Operating system version and Android API level
   - Unique device identifiers (Android Advertising ID / GAID)
   - Device language and locale settings
   - Screen resolution and display density
   - Available storage and memory (for performance optimization)
   - Network connection type (Wi-Fi, mobile data)

b) Gameplay Data
   - Game progress, levels completed, and scores
   - In-game currency earned and spent
   - Session duration, play frequency, and session timestamps
   - Game events (level start, level complete, level failed, upgrades purchased)
   - Features and menus accessed within the Game
   - In-app purchase history (amount, item type, timestamp)

c) Performance and Crash Data
   - Application errors, crash reports, and exception logs
   - Frame rate, load times, and performance metrics
   - SDK version and build information

d) Advertising Data
   - Ad impressions, clicks, and completions
   - Ad interaction data collected by advertising partners
   - Advertising identifier (GAID) for ad personalization purposes

2.2 Information You Provide Voluntarily

We do not require registration or the creation of an account to play Castle Kingdom Season. However, you may voluntarily provide:
   - Customer support inquiries submitted via email to altomediaindonesia@gmail.com
   - Feedback or bug reports submitted through in-game mechanisms or email

2.3 Information We Do NOT Collect

We do NOT collect:
   - Your real name, home address, or national identification numbers
   - Precise GPS location data
   - Camera or microphone input
   - Contacts, call logs, or messages from your device
   - Photos or files stored on your device
   - Biometric data of any kind

6. DATA RETENTION

We retain data for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law:

- Gameplay progress data: Retained for as long as the Game is installed or as long as you have an active account

When data is no longer needed, we take reasonable steps to ensure it is deleted or anonymized.

8. ADVERTISING AND TRACKING

8.1 Types of Ads
Castle Kingdom Season displays the following types of advertisements:
   - Banner ads: Small ads displayed at the top or bottom of the screen
   - Interstitial ads: Full-screen ads displayed between game sessions or level transitions
   - Rewarded video ads: Opt-in video ads that players can watch voluntarily in exchange for in-game rewards (e.g., coins, gems, extra lives)

8.2 Ad Personalization
By default, ad networks may use your advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID) to show you personalized advertisements based on your interests and prior activity across apps and websites. You may opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings:
   - Go to Settings > Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization

8.3 Advertising ID Reset
You can reset your Android Advertising ID at any time in your device settings under Settings > Google > Ads > Reset advertising ID. This will break the link between your previous advertising profile and your device.
